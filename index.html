About Me

A CS Graduate with a Creative Heart

Hi! I'm Pragati Anand, a Computer Science Graduate from New Delhi who found her true calling in visual design and creative media. While my academic journey grounded me in logic and systems thinking, my passion lies in crafting visuals that communicate, evoke emotion, and tell stories.

I am an aspiring junior designer actively exploring tools like Figma, Adobe Photoshop, Premiere Pro, and DaVinci Resolve through self-directed practice. Every project here represents a genuine learning step — not polished perfection, but real growth in progress.

I'm someone who picks up new tools quickly, welcomes honest feedback, and genuinely believes that the best way to learn design is to ship work and keep improving. I'm actively looking for an entry-level creative role where I can contribute, grow, and absorb from experienced designers around me.
